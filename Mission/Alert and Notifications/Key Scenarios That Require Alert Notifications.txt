Key Scenarios That Require Alert Notifications

Action: Define key scenarios that require alert notifications.

Scenario 1: Stock Price Thresholds

Description: Alert when the stock price crosses a user-defined threshold.

Examples:

Price exceeds a set upper limit, indicating a potential breakout.

Price drops below a lower limit, signaling potential losses.

Use Case: Notify users to take immediate action, such as buying or selling stocks.

Scenario 2: Significant Volume Changes

Description: Detect and alert on unusual changes in trading volume within a short timeframe.

Examples:

Sudden spikes in volume that may indicate insider activity or news-driven trades.

Drastic drops in volume that may signify waning investor interest.

Use Case: Provide insights into market activity beyond price movements.

Scenario 3: Predicted High Volatility

Description: Alert when predicted trends indicate increased price volatility.

Examples:

Price patterns signaling potential swings (e.g., Bollinger Band breaches).

Machine learning predictions showing a rise in volatility scores.

Use Case: Warn users of potential risks to help in risk management.

Scenario 4: Market Sentiment Shifts

Description: Notify users when market sentiment changes drastically based on news or social media analysis.

Examples:

Sentiment scores dropping significantly after a major announcement.

Positive sentiment spikes following favorable earnings reports.

Use Case: Provide early warnings for market-driven reactions.

Scenario 5: Technical Indicator Signals

Description: Trigger alerts based on key technical indicator signals.

Examples:

RSI indicates overbought or oversold conditions.

Moving averages showing golden or death crosses.

Use Case: Guide users with actionable insights for technical trading strategies.

Scenario 6: Portfolio-Specific Events

Description: Notify users about changes specific to their portfolio.

Examples:

Alerts for holdings experiencing significant changes in value.

Notifications for stocks hitting user-defined profit or loss targets.

Use Case: Tailor alerts to individual investment strategies and preferences.

Conclusion

By defining these scenarios, the alerting system provides timely and relevant notifications, empowering users to make informed decisions in the dynamic stock market environment.
